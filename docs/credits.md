## Icons

Anta's icon set is built on the shoulders of several excellent open-source icon libraries. We're grateful to the maintainers and contributors of these projects:

- Lucide — the modern, beautifully consistent icon family that supplies the majority of Anta's icons (ISC license).
- Feather — the predecessor that Lucide forked from; a portion of icons trace their lineage back to Cole Bemis's original strokes (MIT license).
- Blueprint by Palantir — additional product-UI glyphs covering domains the previous two don't (Apache License 2.0).

Full license texts and per-icon attribution are reproduced verbatim in the package's NOTICES.md.
